Maintenance on The Skys

With all the monitoring of every simulated floating sky island you'd think there'd be a better way of finding when something goes wrong than pulling up a video feed from the pod. We have alerts for easily measurable issues oxygen concentration or critical distress from any of the subjects (each was implanted with a vitals monitor before they were premitted to enter). But still half of the alerts are a vague "unknown behavior" or "behavior unknown." For some reason, or more aptly for no reason, the unhelpful message is different half the time. I didn't set it up, and I flagged it to may manager two months ago and nothing's changed. I bet they forgot.

At least normally they're easy to dismiss. The last time one of these went off it was because one of the subjects on a sky island started weaving together some grass into a rope. Apparently the automated classifier system has never heard of rope before and thought it might be dangerous. I guess maybe it has a point? But it's not even that, I didn't get a "dangerous behavior" trigger, that's triaged higher, and generally less noisy than the lower triaged alerts. It was "unknown behavior."

Still, that I had time to look at the "lesser" alerts meant none of the islands were facing imminant disaster. This was yet another vague "unknown behavior" (opposed to a "behavior unknown") on island 4f59d223. It's been paritcularly noisy. The subject is a surpisingly determined bearding guy whose been weaving tapesties out the grass on the island. He's been making really impressive sacks and ropes. A few weeks ago he missfired a "jumper imminent" warning when he ran full speed from one side of his island to the other to fly a kite he made. And the kite worked too!

I logged into the video feed to see the guy spinning two sticks together and had been for some time. He was smart about it, making a bow with some grass rope to rotate one faster than he could ever by hand. The tools to be working. I stared for a couple minutes and saw somehow he managed a wiff of smoke. If he kepted up he'd probably start a file... and trigger one of the fire alarms. I was surprised it didn't trigger already.

I don't think I'd ever seen someone doing this before, but neither had anyone flown a kite or made quite as intricate weavings. The closet I'd seen was the Visbon balloons during their new year celebration. Though now, these celebrations have mostly stopped in the city. The man must have been Visbon before becoming a subject on the island.

He kept trying to start his fire, which now I'm pretty sure was so he could make hot air balloons. I didn't want to trigger the fire alarm on him just for that, such and overreaction, and I want to see if he can actually make the balloons. So, I muted the alert on his island.

Notebook Becaues I'm Forgetting Things

July 25, 2026: This notebook is because I think I'm forgetting things. I've never been one to journel but it seems like it's going to become a necessity. This afternoon I got a call from Andrew that he was waiting for me at Gloria's. We were going to meet for my birthday. I somehow forgot it was even my birthday. TODO: actually put things in my calendar.

July 28, 2026: I was two stops from Grand Ave. before I realized I'm not working at the laundry business down there anymore. I was layed off. At least I have savings for a few months, but I should check this every day. I don't trust myself to remember how long I've been unemployed.

July 29, 2026: My name is Sydney. It's written down on all of the ids in my wallet and on my phone and it's what my confused roommate said when I asked. Sydney Harth. Somehow I have to force my brain to mark it with don't forget, because I forgot. Something must be wrong with me. Really when I was on the train to the job I'd lost weeks ago I knew something was wrong with me. I scheduled a checkup with a doctor next week and put it in my calendar (August 4th) with the "critical alert" level of notification. At least when I forget things I seem to notice they're gone. When my name was gone, I knew there was something missing, like I knew I had a name and should know it. There was something on the tip of my tounge but nothing came to me. Though, of course I coudln't know if there was something missing so subtly that I didn't even know it was gone. Yesturday I was nearly walking in the door of a laundrymat to a job I didn't have before realizing something was amiss. At least I think it was a laundrymat, my paystub says (and I'm reading it not remembering), Jansen's Laundry. I guess it could have been some management company for a bunch of landrymats and I was simply heading to an office.

July 30, 2026: Called my parents today, well my mom. My dad was out at a concert with a few of his friends from college. She started with a kind of surprised "why are you calling" so it must have been too long since the last time. The shallow excuse, I forgot, is maybe actually apt for the first time. Though, I didn't tell her about the memories I think I'm loosing. It would make her worry and there's nothing she could do. I told her I got layed off and am looking for a job (I'm not right now) and I don't need money because I have enough savings. That last point was like when the family gets together and each of my aunts and grandmother fight to pay the bill. Finally, I won. Mom was doing well, five more years to retirement she always brings up. That and drama about the her kids, she works in a school. There's this one troublemaker going around who they say has been lighting fires in the bathrooms. The fire alarms had gone off five times in the past month and the principle, Lydia, was about to suspend him. And then she would talk about how Lydia was definately "on the spectrum" and all the parents seemed to hate her but she did a good job with the school.

I didn't have nearly as much to say; I'm mostly by myself at home. Sometimes I talk to my flatmate. I told her about going out with Andrew on my birthday and that I started running every day (I did start) She seemed happy about that, probably because I was also happy about that and she's really good at picking up on what I'm feeling, somehow even over the phone. I also told her I was going to to doctor the next week. Maybe I shouldn't have because Mom immediatly sounded worried and asked "are you all right." I just said it was a regular checkup and I think she took that well. I've never been good at booking these, so she was probably happy I was going at all. We said, goodbye, "I'll try to come back home over the labor day weekend," and hung up. I don't think she noticed anything wrong, which hopefully means most of my memories are still there. And I don't want her to worry.

August 1, 2026: Something about my last entry kept bothering me. Would Mom have noticed if I'd been slowly loosing my memories? It's not like there's much I have to remember when talking to her. I just talk. Like a reflex. Every day when I leave the house for my run I close and lock the door to my apartment but I don't have a single memory locking the door. Maybe whatever's happening to me ate that memory too, but I don't think I'd ever remembered locking the door. I always just knew that I would have done it, and so must have. Really a lot of things are like that; the run I did yesturday where I ran around the pond I just know I did, even if I don't really remember the run itself. Well, I have to have been there, I have the dirty laundry to say I did. Is there really that much of a difference from remembering things and simply knowing they happened? If it weren't for me forgetting my own name, maybe I'd think I was just fine. But sometimes I forget the names of other people I've known for years, a gap I know shouldn't be there but is. It's been this way forever, so I'm maybe just overreacting to forgetting a couple things. That's more of an argument to say something's wrong than something's right, isn't it. I'm just happy I booked that doctor's appointment, best case everything is fine.
